written by a program.

## The joke

> I told my wife I was just popping out for milk — six years later I came
> home with it, and all she said was: "You forgot the bread."

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the one-liner to `/output/entry.txt`. The setup
leans on the classic "popped out for milk" trope, then the payoff flips it:
he comes back after six years, and the only complaint is the forgotten bread.

Run it:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
